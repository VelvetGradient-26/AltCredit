## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, progressive consumer fintech identity centered on alternative credit scoring, financial mobility, and data clarity. It balances the institutional reliability of institutional private banking with the agile, optimistic user experience of next-generation digital neo-banks.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Style Direction:** Modern Neo-Fintech Minimalist with layered surfaces, tactile micro-elevations, and high typographic precision.
- **Brand Personality:** Trustworthy, empowering, analytical, frictionless, and forward-looking.
- **Emotional Impact:** Replaces credit anxiety and opacity with transparent agency, financial confidence, and progressive momentum.
- **Design Metaphor:** "Financial Health as Dynamic Growth" — using rich navigational foundations layered under clear, biometric, and score-driven accents.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to communicate algorithmic clarity, safety, and vitality.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#10B981` / Emerald Mint):** The core indicator of financial wellness, upward mobility, affirmative score movement, and primary conversions. Deepens to `#059669` for hover states and accessible text-on-light contexts.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` / Deep Trust Navy):** The structural anchor. Used for primary typography, authoritative containers, headers, and high-impact card backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / Amber Warning):** Action-required indicators, moderate-risk tiers, verification warnings, and mid-range score bands.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` / Slate):** Mid-tone informational text, metadata labels, and subtle UI strokes. Light neutral foundations are anchored on `#F8FAFC` (Canvas Slate) with pristine `#FFFFFF` card surfaces and `#E2E8F0` structural boundaries.

### Semantic & Data Tokens
- **Score Grade - Exceptional:** `#10B981` (Mint Emerald)
- **Score Grade - Good / Average:** `#0284C7` (Sky Cerulean)
- **Score Grade - Fair / Attention:** `#F59E0B` (Vibrant Amber)
- **Score Grade - High Risk / Action:** `#EF4444` (Crimson Coral)
- **Dark Inversion Base:** `#020617` (Deep Obsidian Navy for elevated dark panels and premium card modes)

## Typography

Typographic scale prioritizes instant scan-ability, numerical precision, and institutional trust.

- **Headline Hierarchy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric forms with tight tracking inject modernity into high-level messaging, score reveals, and section headers.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engine tuned for dense financial transaction details, explanatory disclosures, and form states.
- **Numerical Treatment:** All monetary, credit, and tabular values must enforce tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to guarantee visual alignment across data sets, ledger displays, and score delta tickers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes an intentional, modular grid system designed to shift naturally between a focused single-column financial stream on mobile and a structured multi-column dashboard on desktop.

### Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid with maximum content containment capped at 1280px. Gutters stay at `gutter` (`1.5rem`), outer margins scale dynamically to pad centered viewport framing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column responsive grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `gutter-mobile` (`1rem`) and `margin-mobile` (`1rem`). Bottom navigation bar and sticky financial CTA anchors apply safe-area paddings.
- **Layout Cadence:** Vertical distances rely strictly on `4px` base increments. Component groupings adhere to `space-sm` for related micro-labels, `space-md` for internal panel padding, and `space-xl` between modular financial feature blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of subtle physical layering and crisp low-contrast outlines.

### Depth Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Layer 1 (Standard Card & Container):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFC`. Bound by a 1px solid hairline border in `#E2E8F0`, accompanied by a diffused micro-ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces / Score Breakdown):** Elevated hover states, bottom sheets, and dropdown selectors use a dual shadow: `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 20px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Fixed floating states shielded with an ambient blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px) bg-slate-900/40`), elevated with `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)`.
- **Navy Accent Pods:** Featured credit cards and tier highlights invert using `#0F172A` with an internal high-contrast top border highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces modern, soft geometry that feels approachable and warm without compromising utilitarian rigor.

- **Base Radius:** `0.5rem` (8px) applied to input controls, interactive tags, and list cell items.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) applied to primary cards, modal containers, and dashboard metric pods.
- **Pill Radius:** `9999px` reserved strictly for status chips, numerical delta indicators (e.g., `+18 pts`), and primary action buttons to enhance tactile responsiveness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), `#10B981` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight (`600`). On hover, transitions cleanly to `#059669` with an interactive elevation lift. Active state compresses scale to `0.98`.
- **Secondary (Navy):** Pill shape, `#0F172A` surface, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle hover lift.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Borderless `#F8FAFC` background with `#0F172A` text, shifting to `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Cards & Data Panels
- **Standard Metric Card:** Pure white background, `1rem` corner radius, `1px` solid `#E2E8F0` border, `1.5rem` internal padding. Displays metric label in uppercase `label-sm` (`#64748B`), large tabular value, and contextual health tags.
- **Dark Tier Highlight Card:** Deep Trust Navy (`#0F172A`) container with emerald micro-gradients or mint badges to display credit score breakthroughs and exclusive borrowing terms.

### Chips & Health Indicators
- **Score Grade Chip:** Compact pill with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
  - *Positive:* Emerald background tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`) with `#059669` text.
  - *Attention:* Amber background tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`) with `#D97706` text.
  - *Neutral:* Slate background tint (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)`) with `#475569` text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, rounded `0.5rem`, `1px` solid `#CBD5E1` border, `#FFFFFF` background. Text in `#0F172A` with `#94A3B8` placeholder. Focused states feature a crisp 2px primary ring (`#10B981`) with zero border color clashes.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `0.5rem` / circle border with custom SVG checks in `#10B981`.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Single horizontal lines segmented by `1px` borders in `#F1F5F9`.
- Includes an icon slot (40px circular background tinted to category), transaction/metric title, contextual timestamp, and right-aligned tabular numeric currency or score impact.

### Specialized Fintech Elements
- **Radial Score Meter:** Precision multi-segment arc gauge with glow effects on current point position, paired with centered `score-metric` typography.
- **Alternative Data Factor Row:** Visual bar showing category weight (e.g., Utility Bills, Cash Flow, Recurring Subscriptions) with dynamic fractional progress indicators in `#10B981`.
